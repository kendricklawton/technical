### Question 1
During compensation negotiation for a Senior Platform Engineer offer, the recruiter says: "This is our final offer; take it or leave it by tomorrow." What is the recommended negotiation strategy?

A) Decline the offer instantly.  
B) Immediately accept out of fear of losing the offer.  
C) Reply with an angry email threatening to expose them on social media.  
D) Remain calm, professional, and anchored in market data; thank them warmly for the offer, reiterate your high enthusiasm for the role and team, and politely request a 48-hour extension while highlighting specific value-add points (competing offers or verified total compensation benchmarks).  

<details>
<summary><strong>View Answer & Explanation</strong></summary>

**Correct Answer:** D  
**Explanation:** Negotiation is a collaboration, not an adversarial fight. Professionalism, gratitude, and clear articulation of market benchmarks or competing timelines allow recruiters to advocate on your behalf to the compensation committee without alienating the hiring team.
</details>

### Question 4

<summary><strong>View Answer & Explanation</strong></summary>

### Question 8
What is the recommended approach to discussing salary history during initial recruiter screens?

A) Immediately reveal your exact current salary and bonuses.  
B) Demand double what the job posting lists.

<details>
<summary><strong>View Answer & Explanation</strong></summary>
